1. Who we are

Star Game is a free Flutter Android game published by Bollojula Srikanth (package name com.stargame.app). It is designed for a general audience, including children and families. You can create or join an online room to play a real-time multiplayer card-passing game with friends or family, play a local game against the computer, or play solo mini-games in Time Pass (currently Squash the Bugs).

The app does not require a user account, does not show advertisements, and does not process payments or real money.

2. Information we collect

What is collected depends on how you use the app. For online play, we collect only the minimum needed to run the game.

Online multiplayer (Create Room or Join Room). To run a shared game, the app stores the following in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore:

  • Display name: the name you type, or a suggested random name you can change, before you create or join a room. This name is shown to other players in the same room. Please do not use a real full name, especially a child’s real full name; a first name, nickname, or the suggested random name is enough.
  • Player identifier: a random ID generated on your device for that session (it is not a Google or other login, and it is not saved on your device for later sessions).
  • Room and game data: the 4-digit room code, who created the room, who has joined, game status, cards and turns, scores and finishing places, the current app version of the room and of each player, and similar session details needed to keep everyone in sync.
  • Emoji reactions: if you send an emoji during a game, the app stores the emoji, your player ID, your display name, and a timestamp in that game’s emoji list. The app is designed to drop older reactions after a short time.

On your device only. The app may store a sound mute setting and your Time Pass personal best scores in your device’s app storage. Play vs Computer and Time Pass games run on your device and are not written to our Firebase database.

The app does not:

  • Create or require user accounts, or use Firebase Authentication
  • Collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information
  • Include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • Sell your personal information

The only Android permission the app requests is Internet access, which is needed for online multiplayer and to open this privacy policy.

When the app talks to Firebase, Google’s systems may also process technical information that is typical of internet traffic, such as IP address and basic device or request metadata, under Google’s own privacy policy. We do not use a separate analytics or advertising SDK to profile you.

3. How we use information

We use this information only to:

  • Create and join rooms, and keep the online game in sync between players
  • Show display names, scores, turns, and emoji reactions to people in the same room or game
  • Make sure players in a room are on a compatible app version
  • Remember your mute setting and Time Pass best scores on your device
  • Allow the publisher to maintain the service, including clearing old room and game records

We do not sell this information, and we do not use it for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use or share data for advertising.

  • Other players in your room: your display name, game actions, scores, and any emoji you send are visible to the other people in that room or game. That is required for multiplayer play.
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore): online room, game, and emoji data is stored and synchronized through Google’s Firebase service, which we use only as a service provider to run the multiplayer features. Google processes that data as a service provider and under Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security documentation.

We may also disclose information if required by law or in response to a valid request from a public authority.

5. Storage and retention

Online room and game data is stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore for as long as it is needed to run the game and maintain recent sessions. Old rooms and games are periodically cleared from the database, and the display names, player IDs, game state, and emoji reactions in those records are deleted at that time. Some unfinished or stuck sessions may remain until the next cleanup or until a deletion request is completed.

Emoji reactions are intended to be short-lived and are cleaned up after a short period.

Information stored only on your device (mute preference and Time Pass best scores) stays there until you change it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete online room or game records that other players may still be using, or that remain in Firebase until they are cleaned up or deleted on request.

6. Security

Game data sent to Firebase is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to the database is limited by Firebase security rules. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information stored in the cloud.

7. Children’s privacy

Star Game is designed for a general audience, including children, and is intended to be suitable for players of all ages, including family play.

We collect only the minimum information needed to play. For an online room, that is a display name (please do not use a real full name, especially a child’s real full name; a nickname or the suggested random name is enough), a random player ID that lasts only for that session, the game state needed to keep everyone in the room in sync, and any emoji reactions sent during the game. Play vs Computer and Time Pass run on the device and are not written to our Firebase database.

The app does not require an account. It does not include advertisements, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and it does not collect location or contact data. We do not sell personal information and we do not use any of this information for advertising. Google Firebase is used only as a service provider to run the online multiplayer features.

Parents and guardians can request deletion of a child’s or family’s online game data by emailing seven.srikanth@gmail.com with the room code, the approximate date, and the display name that was used. We will delete the related room and game records that we can reasonably identify. This data is also deleted when old rooms and games are periodically cleared from the database.

8. Your choices and deletion

You can use a suggested random display name instead of a personal name, and you can change the name before you create or join a room. Please do not enter a real full name. You can play Time Pass or Play vs Computer without creating an online room.

The app does not have an in-app account or a built-in “delete my data” button, because there is no sign-in account. Players, parents, or guardians who want online data deleted can email seven.srikanth@gmail.com with the room code, approximate date, and the display name that was used, and we will delete the related room and game records that we can reasonably identify. Online data is also deleted when old rooms and games are periodically cleared.

You may uninstall the app at any time to remove the local settings and scores stored on your device.

If you have a question or a request about privacy, including any rights that may apply under the law where you live, contact us at the same email address.

9. Third-party services

Online features use Google Firebase Cloud Firestore only as a service provider to run multiplayer. When the app sends data to Firebase, Google’s privacy policy also applies to information Google receives in the course of providing that service:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
